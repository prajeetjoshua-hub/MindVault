# MindVault progress report — 13 September 2026

Owner: prajeetjoshua-hub. Local functional prototype; Android acceptance pending.

## What is built

The public GitHub Pages product demonstration remains separate from the functional app and private diagnostics dashboard. The desktop milestone is committed locally under the user's configured identity; no installable Android release has been produced.

The React Native/Expo app has the Quiet Forest theme, animated squirrel, Home, Companion, guided Check-in, Journal/history, Settings and Help screens. Styles and processing layers are separate. Typed conversation works on desktop. Check-in creates an editable message. Users can choose brief/balanced replies, gentle/direct tone and listening/reflection/planning. History is off by default; memories require explicit entry. Export has a preview; deletion removes conversations and linked memories. Browser storage is session memory only.

## Conversation improvements

Replies now distinguish ordinary purchases, achievements, broken purchases, worry, sadness, overwhelm, anger, bereavement, exam disappointment, difficulty thinking and feedback about the companion. Listening mode avoids immediately prescribing a plan. Responses use authored alternatives to reduce repetition. Grief and distress receive appropriate empathy rather than forced cheerfulness. These remain finite structured replies, not unrestricted language understanding.

The reported safety loop has been corrected for the tested sequence. “I didn't hurt myself” is no longer treated as fresh positive harm evidence. Explicit reassurance together with denial of harm can end a pending non-acute safety clarification. A vague “I am fine” receives a shorter clarification instead of the full emergency paragraph. New intent or reported injury takes priority. Reassurance does not casually clear a reported overdose or injury. These transitions use self-report and limited patterns; they cannot establish that a person is safe.

Non-immediate safety expressions ask what happened and whether the words describe overwhelm or thoughts of ending life. Immediate danger, imminent intent, injury and overdose bypass that delay. MEDIUM distress receives relevant support and questions without automatically presenting an emergency response. The internal safety check always runs before wording is selected; it is not postponed until a second message.

## Processing and diagnostics

Input → normalisation → full-message evidence extraction → deterministic policy → conversation orchestration → structured support/eligible local model → output gate → selected local storage. Personalisation supplies consented preferences and relevant memories. Cancellation and app lifecycle handling are separate services.

Policy 0.1.3-experimental uses deduplicated demonstration contributions: persistence 2 (with distress context), disrupted functioning 2, overwhelm 1, coping difficulty 1. Scores 0–1 select LOW; 2+ selects MEDIUM unless safety/uncertainty overrides. These are unvalidated engineering thresholds, not risk percentages. Four support groups, two safety groups and one ambiguity expression exist; there is no reviewed 100,000-entry corpus. English only; Tanglish remains unsupported.

The local dashboard receives real redacted execution events and shows evidence IDs, score contributions, route, coverage, model permission, storage outcome and recorded regression results. It requires explicit pairing. Raw chat and generated text are omitted; remaining metadata can still be sensitive. The receiver keeps traces in memory, uses expiring pairing credentials and rejects unapproved event fields. It is not the public GitHub page.

## Help and calling

The app includes India emergency 112, Child Helpline 1098, Women's Helpline 181 and Tele-MANAS 14416 (alternative 1800 891 4416). Women's Helpline coverage notes the official West Bengal exception. Sources checked on 13 September 2026: [112](https://112.gov.in/), [Child Helpline](https://spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/detail-mission-vatsalya/faqs), [Women's Helpline](https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/help/faqs), [Tele-MANAS](https://dghs.mohfw.gov.in/national-mental-health-programme.php).

SOS selects a confirmation and then offers to open the telephone dialler. The app does not silently call, send location or deliver alerts. Numbers are readable offline; calling requires telephone service. Browser testing stopped before opening the dialler; no emergency call was placed.

## Verification completed

- All 120 automated regression tests passed with zero failures.
- TypeScript checking passed; production web export of the functional prototype passed.
- Browser verified: mixed monitor/dying message asks context and a direct safety question; “now i am good i didnt hurt myself” ends that pending loop; the next listening request receives a normal listening reply.

- The suite includes different harm verb forms, whitespace/case variants, negation, contradictory intent, positive wording mixed with danger, persistent ordinary positive updates, long-message coverage, model gates, preferences, cancellation and state transitions.
- Food and relationship phrase families now have dedicated authored modules and replay coverage. See [CONTEXT-COVERAGE.md](CONTEXT-COVERAGE.md) for the public references used to sanity-check those categories.
- Model gating now preserves specific LOW authored replies and invokes Qwen only for MEDIUM support or a generic LOW fallback; safety and uncertainty routes never call the model.
- Earlier desktop checks covered dashboard traces, export preview/download and separation of public and functional builds.

Passing fixtures does not demonstrate clinical validity, universal comprehension, teenager suitability or reliable detection of every emergency. Broader reviewed evaluation and usability work remain necessary.

## Written but awaiting phone verification

Native adapters implement SQLCipher encrypted SQLite, SecureStore keys, system authentication with device-credential fallback, background locking, native sharing and a local model adapter. They have not been demonstrated on the Samsung F15. Browser checks do not verify native encryption or device unlock.

Gemma 3 1B was downloaded, integrity-checked and rejected after real conversation review. Qwen3 4B Instruct 2507 is the current desktop candidate and passed the focused transcript review; CPU latency, RAM, temperature, automatic reload and phone compatibility remain pending. Offline voice transcription is not implemented.
